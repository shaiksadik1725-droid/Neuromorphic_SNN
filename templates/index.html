How a row becomes a recommendation

Pipeline
  1. 01

    Standardise

    Each feature is centred and scaled with the fitted StandardScaler — the same one used during training.

  2. 02

    Rate-encode

    Values pass through a sigmoid, then become Bernoulli spikes replicated across 30 timesteps.

  3. 03

    Integrate & fire

    Four leaky layers (256 → 128 → 64 → 22) accumulate membrane potential and emit spikes at every step.

  4. 04

    Vote & average

    Spike counts are softmaxed; multiple stochastic runs are averaged for a stable confidence estimate.
